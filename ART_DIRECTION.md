# WINDRUN — Art foundation targets

**North star:** the user-approved painterly key art with rolling verdant hills,
blue skies full of luminous clouds, tall soft meadows, a golden-scarfed runner,
and distant ancient rings. This is **concept art**, not a screenshot or FPS promise.

## What version 1.2 tackles

1. **Grass silhouettes:** alpha-cut illustrated curves instead of bare sharp
   triangle geometry; two crossing cards per instance.
2. **Sky presence:** panoramic clouds near camera-visible horizon plus transparent
   painted atmospheric cards for missing cloud angles.
3. **Lighting/terrain:** less washed-out hillside colors; broad, organic green
   gradients and more visible valley shadows.
4. **Trees:** irregularly shaded crowns, differentiated foliage colors and thin
   angled branches, retaining sparse placement.
5. **Camera:** visually readable runner at 200+ km/h while preserving speed.

## Upcoming artistic work (not included)

- Fully rigged imported skinned runner with polished motion capture or animator-made clips.
- Mesh-based leaf clusters on more varied tree silhouettes.
- Streamed distant river, cliffs and sculptural scenic regions.
- A proper sky/atmosphere system with cloud shadows and smooth time-of-day blending.

The current hardware aim remains Intel Core 5 / integrated Intel GPU, 16GB RAM,
with adjustable vegetation density and a *target* of 30–60 FPS.
