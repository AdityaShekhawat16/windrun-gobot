# WINDRUN — Dreamy Painterly Art Direction

A sunlit, slightly fantastical third-person world of huge, rounded green hills.
The player is a cream-coated traveler in a golden scarf, sprinting and jumping
vast distances. Natural motion and deliberate color composition matter more
than photorealistic surfaces or a large object count.

The user-approved images show the artistic **target**, not the current in-engine
quality. v1.2 moves the foundation toward that target by improving the sky,
grass silhouettes, colors, sparse tree details, and speed framing.

Next major artistic challenge: a proper rigged character, varied tree meshes,
landmark art, cloud shadows, and a coherent regional landscape composition.
